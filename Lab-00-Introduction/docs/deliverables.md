# Lab 00 Setup Checklist

**Lab 00 and its outputs are optional.** The following checklist can help you
confirm that your environment works. You may keep the files locally in
`Lab-00-Introduction/submission/` for reference or troubleshooting; they are
not part of the required lab deliverables.

1. **Console output**  
   - `hello_cpp_output.txt` — capture the output of  
     ```
     ./build/src/core/examples/hello-simulator
     ```  
     It must contain at least the line  
     ```
     Hello Simulator
     ```

2. **NetAnim XML & screenshot**  
   - `lab0_cpp_anim.xml` — the XML you generated in Part 2.  
   - `anim_screenshot_cpp.png` — a screenshot of NetAnim displaying your two-node "Hello" simulation.
