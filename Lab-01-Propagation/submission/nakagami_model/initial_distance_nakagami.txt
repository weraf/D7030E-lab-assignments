RxSensitivity           = -92 dBm      -> 6.3096e-13 W

Formula (Friis received power, solved for d):
  d_i = lambda * sqrt( (Pt * Gt * Gr) / ((4*pi)^2 * Pr * L) )

  d_i = 0.057875 * sqrt(2.00254e9)

  d_i ≈ 2589.89 m
